Frequently Asked Questions

Everything worth knowing before you deploy. Still curious? Ask our assistant anything, in your own words.
What is Oversite?
Oversite Customs is the ER:LC design product from Oversite: a managed Discord bot plus web dashboard that runs a Roblox ER:LC (Emergency Response: Liberty County) design server end to end. Commissions and order tickets, designer portfolios, package storefronts, payments, a credits economy, Roblox verification and group sync, and join logs all live in one place, with nothing to self-host.
Sell me on why Oversite
Looking at the bot market there are three ways you can take. Making a bot yourself, this could be through AI spending all that money on credits then keeping up to date on the bot weekly when Discord makes changes. Paying for a custom bot usually priced at $30-50 then $20 to host it and any future updates or bug fixes are another $20-40. Or you can just go with Oversite: one time payment, free hosting for life, and any future updates we make get automatically applied to your bot for completely free.
What can Oversite do for an ERLC design server?
It handles the full commission workflow: order tickets, an order-status board, designer portfolios, package storefronts, payments, credits, Roblox verification, group sync, and join logs. A design server can take, track, and deliver work like a real business from one dashboard.
Do I have to host anything myself?
No. Every bot runs on Oversite's infrastructure. There's no VPS to rent, no process to keep alive, and no token to babysit. You connect your server and we run it.
How fast can I get set up?
Minutes. Authorize the bots, choose what you want enabled, and our auto-deploy pipeline has you live in under sixty seconds, all from the dashboard.
